Who we are

Why Damana exists

Damana Health Support exists to improve access to healthcare for people facing language, cultural and communication barriers. We help individuals understand, navigate and engage with healthcare services so they can make informed decisions about their health.

  • Dignity first. Every person is met with patience, respect and genuine care.
  • Culturally rooted. Support delivered by people who share your language and lived experience.
  • Always free to those we help. Funded by the generosity of donors like you.
